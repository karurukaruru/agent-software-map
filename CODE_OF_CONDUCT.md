# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Focus criticism on ideas and evidence, not people.
- Accept corrections and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Protect credentials, recordings, private paths, and personal information.
- Do not misrepresent documentation-based work as live-tested evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

For sensitive reports, use GitHub private vulnerability reporting when available. Otherwise, open an issue requesting a private contact channel without including sensitive details. All reports will be reviewed fairly and as promptly as practical.
